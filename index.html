What it is

Not a diary. Not a group chat. Somewhere warmer, in between.

Every entry in revery is one of two things — and you choose which, every time.

Solo

Private, for your eyes only. The thoughts you're still working out, the moment you want to remember exactly as it felt — nobody sees it unless you decide to.

Shared

Written together. Invite the people who were there, and everyone can add their own side of the same memory — so it's never just one person's version.

How it works

Three small habits, one shared history.

Capture

Write a moment alone, or bring in the people who were part of it. Takes less time than opening your camera roll.

Organize

See your memories however makes sense — scroll through them in a feed, or lay them across the year on a calendar. Filter to just yours, just shared, or everything at once.

Look back, together

The point isn't just writing it down. revery hands back what you wrote a year ago, or five, on this day — so you open it again, together.

Trust

Your memories are yours. We built it that way on purpose.

revery is designed so the people you didn't invite in — including us — simply can't read what you write.

  • End-to-end encryptedYour entries and photos are encrypted on your device before they ever leave it. revery can't read them, and neither can anyone else.
  • On-device safety checksContent moderation happens locally on your phone, not on a server somewhere reading your memories.
  • You control accessNothing is shared until you invite someone, and you can change your mind at any time.
